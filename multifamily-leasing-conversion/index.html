The invisible middle of the funnel

A prospect can be interested without being ready to schedule.

Property websites are good at generating research behavior: floor-plan views, amenity browsing, repeat visits, and time on neighborhood pages. Most of that intent remains anonymous until a visitor completes a contact or tour form.

A question to a resident is a smaller commitment. It gives a prospect a reason to identify themselves while addressing the personal hesitation that may be keeping them from the official conversion path.

What attribution answers

Measure a resident program against leases, not chat volume.

  • Which named prospects began with a resident conversation
  • Which resident profiles and room types attract relevant questions
  • How many resident-chat prospects toured or signed
  • Which recurring objections appear before a prospect converts
  • What the per-lease success fee corresponds to on the invoice

Conversation intelligence

Learn from the questions without exposing the people asking them.

Private conversations create honest signals only if the participants trust the channel. ResThreads therefore does not provide management with transcript access.

Instead, teams receive aggregated, anonymized themes—such as repeated questions about noise, packages, parking, pets, or working from home. Those themes can inform property content, chatbot knowledge, sales enablement, and operational fixes while protecting conversation privacy.
