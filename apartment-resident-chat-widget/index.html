A different kind of chat

Your leasing tools know the property. Residents know the experience.

A conventional apartment chatbot can share rent, availability, policies, amenities, and tour times. A leasing agent can explain the application process. Neither can credibly answer what it feels like to take work calls from a fourth-floor one-bedroom or use the package room during the holidays.

ResThreads routes those experiential questions to residents who opted in to answer them. The prospect gets a candid peer perspective while your existing leasing tools keep doing the jobs they do best.

Common questions

What property teams ask before installing resident chat.

Does this replace our AI leasing chatbot?

No. ResThreads is designed to sit alongside it. Your AI assistant handles factual and transactional questions; residents handle personal, experiential questions.

Can management read the conversations?

No. Management receives the prospect record, attribution, and anonymized themes—not private conversation transcripts.

How does it reach our leasing workflow?

Guest cards arrive through the community’s leasing inbox, which major property CRMs already ingest. A direct PMS implementation is not required to launch.
