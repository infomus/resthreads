About ResThreads

Software for the questions a leasing page cannot answer.

ResThreads gives prospective renters a private, moderated way to speak with verified residents—then gives multifamily teams the attribution and anonymized insight they need to improve leasing.

Why we exist

Renters can find facts anywhere. Confidence still comes from people.

Property websites, leasing teams, and AI assistants are good at explaining availability, policies, amenities, and next steps. Prospects still wonder what daily life actually feels like: whether a building is quiet enough to work from home, how the package room works in practice, or what surprised someone after move-in.

Residents can answer those questions from experience. ResThreads makes that conversation safe, private, measurable, and easy to add to an existing property website.

What we build

A resident voice with operational guardrails.

1

A prospect-facing widget

Verified resident profiles and private chat can appear inline on a property page or in a compact corner panel.

2

A managed ambassador program

Communities invite residents, set rewards, and give participants a clear role without asking them to become leasing agents.

3

An attributable leasing channel

Guest cards, tour progress, and signed leases connect back to the resident conversation that helped create them.

Trust by design

Candid for the prospect. Useful for the property. Private by default.

  • Management receives lead attribution and anonymized themes, not private conversation transcripts.
  • Automated screening checks for fair-housing issues, abuse, solicitation, and attempts to exchange personal contact details.
  • Residents speak from their own experience; pricing, availability, and leasing decisions stay with the leasing team.
  • ResThreads works alongside existing AI, CRM, and resident-referral programs instead of forcing a replacement.
